PRESENTATION PRECEDENCE — V1
1. Active higher-authority ownership excludes ambient runtime: Away/unavailable form, Social Presence, pending Arrival, current Item Usage, active user-directed Hall scene focus, interaction/focus/explore ownership.
2. While the ambient controller owns the resident, its transient standing visual is passed explicitly to the active Hall map marker.
3. With no transient pose, getMapCatVisualPresentation uses getMapCatPose and statusPosture.resolveMapPose, including the presentation-only legacy fallback.
Historical Hall scene records alone do not permanently block ambient activity. An accepted new scene cancels the affected runtime.
Active user-directed Hall focus is re-evaluated when its existing 20-minute ownership window expires; its expiry timer is removed on unmount.
