FOOT ANCHOR TRANSITION PROOF — V1
The ambient wrapper owns projected world foot coordinates. A pose switch changes only the child image and its renderer-derived anchor CSS. makeGroundAnchorPlacement(frame, 72) is used for both baseline and transient Standing sprites.
