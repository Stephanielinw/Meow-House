REST TO STANDING CONTRACT — V1
After the ordinary bounded idle decision selects a legal neighbor, the runtime reserves that target. Structured sitting, crouching, and lying then enter transitioning/preparing-standing while their resting image remains displayed. The existing Cat Renderer prepares a standing sprite using the same visual config.
When ready, ambientMotionPose becomes standing. The displayed Standing image holds for 350 ms before movement can start. No pose art or stored Status mutation occurs. A failed visual preparation or 5 s readiness timeout releases the target and returns to the normal idle retry.
Structured standing uses the direct movement path without a rest-to-stand hold. Missing/invalid structured posture remains stationary.
