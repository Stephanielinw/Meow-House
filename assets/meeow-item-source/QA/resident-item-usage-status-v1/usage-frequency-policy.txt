For each requested HOME resident in an ordinary Status Sync AI generation: require at least one currently owned usable item, no active cooldown, then roll once. Failure leaves the ordinary status prompt unchanged.
Cooldown authority is derived from max(lastUsedAt) across all valid current and historical bonds for that resident. Invalid bond history blocks usage conservatively. No separate mutable cooldown field is stored.
Holiday context does not change chance or cooldown.
