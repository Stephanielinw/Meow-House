Weight = max(1, 6 + familiarity + 2*fondness + (cherished ? 4 : 0)).
Use one weighted random draw among eligible physical instances; do not always select the highest weight. No personality profile, semantic bonus, boredom, recency penalty, or novelty bonus is used.
Before commit, compare exact object reference, uniqueId, sourceCatalogId, and semantics. If the item changed or disappeared, invalidate the event, do not substitute, and apply an ordinary neutral status.
