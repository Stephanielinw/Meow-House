KEY INVARIANT
todayEvent and todayEventContext are reactive refs, but neither is directly rendered as a persistent holiday component. Returning todayEvent from setup (~29272) is not the same as using it in the template. The visible Hall text is one shared mutable alfredMessage that many ordinary flows replace.
