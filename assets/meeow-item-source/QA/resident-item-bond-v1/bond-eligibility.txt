Bond eligibility uses the resident gift ownership policy, not Backpack tab placement.
Eligible: toy category/type, actual collectible type, souvenir type, including unclassified collectible objects.
Ineligible: Food category, literal letter type, and non-item records.
Semantic classification is not required for owning an eligible object or having a bond.
An item-use caller can require known usable semantics with requireUsableSemantics.
