BOUNDARIES AND FAILURES
- 2026-03-12: ordinary day, no banner or interaction holiday block.
- America/Los_Angeles: 2026-09-26T06:59:59Z is local 2026-09-25, Mid-Autumn active.
- America/Los_Angeles: 2026-09-26T07:00:00Z is local 2026-09-26, Mid-Autumn inactive.
- Local 00:00 refresh and visibility/focus refresh re-evaluate and reschedule.
- Device clock/date change is adopted at the next visibility/focus refresh.
- 03:00 operational-day constant remains in other game systems; holiday module does not read it.
- Negative lunar months (leap months) do not match ordinary lunar festival rules.
- Lunar conversion failure preserves Gregorian results and reports an error code.
- A contrived Gregorian+lunar collision preserves rule order and lunar primary precedence.

INTERACTION CONTEXT
- Ordinary date: no holiday block.
- Mid-Autumn/Christmas: current structured ID, name and greeting only.
- Phone Direct Reply and Hall direct use the same dispatch-time holiday snapshot.
- The Phone sidecar receives its own labeled block; Hall-only details remain outside it.
- Later requests after a date refresh receive the new local date's context.
