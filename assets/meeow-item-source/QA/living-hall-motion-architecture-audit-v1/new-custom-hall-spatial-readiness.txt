Current New/Custom Hall generation records worldview/theme and resident assignment but no unique background/layout/furniture, walkable map, spawn or depth metadata. Shared existing room art is a valid current presentation. A future Hall with bespoke art must supply a spatial sidecar referenced by Hall layout ID/version; if absent, retain current static presentation and do not permit autonomous roam. Do not ask AI to infer collision from a Hall's source text.

Curator private Hall is not authored art. Treat it as a greenfield case and do not require it for the first Living Hall slice. Day-one art/data contract: recommended 1024×1024 canvas and matching 16-px visual grid; >=45% traversable floor with >=30% one connected footprint-safe component; two separated safe spawn slots, two interaction clearings and reachable POI anchors; walls/furniture/hot/wet/no-go boundaries; foreground occluder PNGs or reviewed regions; depth zones and foot-Y sorting compatibility; >=96 world-px clear corridors around a 32-world-px foot radius; 112×104 native cat frame projected to roughly 96–112 world px; one versioned metadata sidecar containing coordinate size, obstacles, floor, POIs, slots, spawn, occluder and asset references. Responsive display must project world coordinates uniformly into the square stage and scale sprite size with the stage, with no viewport-CSS-position save.

These numbers are recommended art acceptance targets, not descriptions of existing Curator art. Existing six percentage anchors express private-room status/presence only and cannot be treated as a walkable plan. A future Curator design may revise the targets through visual review before production; this audit creates neither room art nor a simulated Curator map.
