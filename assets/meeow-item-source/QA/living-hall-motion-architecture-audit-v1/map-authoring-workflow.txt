Living Hall spatial/motion architecture audit V1 — map authoring workflow

Evidence: current hardcoded room registry js/meeow-map.js:1-116 and existing 1024 PNGs under assets/meeow-map; no spatial sidecar, mask, obstacle editor or map-version contract. Current README is partly stale and should not be used as a production validator.

Recommendation: one versioned, data-only spatial sidecar/registry per authored room, reviewed together with its layered PNGs. Fields: room/map ID, version, world width/height, asset references, floor polygons or coarse traversable cells, blocked/unsafe obstacle polygons expanded by foot clearance, connected components/optional waypoint edges, spawn points, POI IDs/slots/allowed posture/facing/approach, furniture depth anchors, foreground occluder regions and projection/scale rules. Keep machine IDs stable and mapPoint aliases as compatibility metadata. A development overlay can later show walkable cells, collision footprint, routes, POI slots, occluders and cat-scale boxes over the actual art; do not embed invisible topology in AI prose or sprite alpha.

Existing-map retrofit: trace actual visual floor and blockers over each 1024 image, validate every current POI as floor/elevated/static-only, author conservative reachable loops first, and split/clip foreground furniture only where crossings will be permitted. Do not migrate residents or change current map art just to establish a safe living-rug loop.

Future Curator greenfield: deliver the sidecar in the same review package as the first art, not after the room is drawn. Recommend 1024×1024 sRGB pixel-art canvas matching current 16-px visual grid, with declared coordinates and version. Author >=45% of total canvas as traversable floor and >=30% as one connected safe roaming component after obstacle and 32-world-px foot-radius clearance; these are design acceptance targets, not measured values of existing maps. Include at least two safe 96-world-px-clear interaction clearings, two non-overlapping spawn slots, reachable POI approach points, explicit no-go furniture/wall/heat/water boundaries, one resident scale reference (112×104 native frame projected to about 96–112 world px width), foreground occluder layers and sortable foot-Y depth zones. Test at narrow and wide viewport sizes using one world->stage projection; never store CSS px as world data. These targets must be reviewed against first Curator art when it is eventually authored. No art or mockup is produced now.
