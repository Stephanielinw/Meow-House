Resident contract is tested once per identity: visual resolves; actual config renders Standing/Sitting/Crouching/Lying; frame has finite ground anchor. All 55 pass. Surface result is then a shared requirement, not resident-specific logic. A resting structured pose can transition locally; missing/invalid structured pose fails closed.
R = ordinary floor/rug: resident-capable, spatial metadata or entry needed except exact supported living-rug placements. S = sofa/rest: resident-capable, only exact living sofa slots move now; other rest surfaces need authoring. E = elevated shelf: resident-capable, no transition/depth route. C = climber/scratch: resident-capable, no authored transition graph (scratch map mismatch needs review). U = special/unsupported: resident-capable but movement blocked by spatial contract. The symbols are derived once from shared resident rendering plus surface contracts; they are not 275 rules.
