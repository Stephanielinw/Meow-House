CURRENT SUPPORT AND FUTURE AUTHORING LEVEL
Current support requires: active gotham::living, CAT present in Hall, point presentation foot exactly equal to an ambientSlots foot, and component mapping in ambientEntrySlot. Only living-rug and sofa-seat can produce entries, and only for some colocated placement indexes. Others remain static Hall markers.
Levels classify the next missing contract: L0 unknown; L1 static only; L2 ordinary same-surface roaming after metadata; L3 exact/special surface graph; L4 authored transition edge; L5 visual/depth/art or anchor reconciliation first. Existing supported points still have a level describing future expansion; level is not a current-support boolean.
