FUTURE CURATOR HALL (GREENFIELD)
Curator room currently uses CURATOR_ROOM_ANCHORS and a separate marker presentation in index.html, not a 1024-square Living Hall room with layered art. Missing Curator Hall art is expected, not a regression.
