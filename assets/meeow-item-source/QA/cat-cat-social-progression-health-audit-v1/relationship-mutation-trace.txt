Failure/neutral classifications
- No scene or one eligible participant: no relationship call.
- Ambient invalid scene, form-unsafe quarantine, stale response, absent accepted Hall record: no relationship call.
- Valid scene with missing/empty/invalid directional proposals: scene commits and pair exposure records, but no warmth/trust/tension change. Invalid entries are filtered by normalizer rather than rejecting the scene; no aggregate rejection count is recorded.
- Valid directional proposal at dimension cap or with no effective tag change: stage returns no event. The receipt can still mark the namespace complete. No feedback explains the zero result to USER.
- Accepted scene calls apply, but caller ignores its Boolean return. A rare transaction/partial-receipt failure is logged; the scene may remain visible without relationship consequences. This is a diagnosability/integrity gap, not an observed failure rate.
- Relationship mutation uses a synchronous rollback envelope; the existing relationship fixture verifies normal application, idempotency, detached/rejected record protection, rollback and reload-shaped normalization.
