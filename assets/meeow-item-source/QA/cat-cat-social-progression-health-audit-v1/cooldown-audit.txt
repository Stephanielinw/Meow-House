Effective worst case: with autoUpdate disabled or Hall not contextually viewed, autonomous ambient scene opportunities are zero indefinitely. With a continuously viewed Hall, automatic checks occur about three times per hour, but a successful request that completes after its tick resets the 20-minute gate and makes the immediately next regular tick too early. Under regular timing this produces roughly one accepted automatic request every 40 minutes (about 1.5/hour), before other gates. Any accepted scene-less Hall sync can postpone the next ambient opportunity further. Attention has zero opportunity indefinitely without USER direct chat; following a delivered ambient bid, the Hall waits at least 45 minutes and that candidate 90 minutes for another ambient bid. These mechanisms stack but apply to different producers. No dedicated global or pair cooldown suppresses substantive relationshipDeltas once a valid Hall scene is accepted.
