ACTIVE SOCIAL PRESENCE — REACHABILITY AND CONTRIBUTION

Intended behavior from current code: after a successful, plain direct Homepage chat, a second same-Hall resident may notice a public cue or sustained USER attention to the active resident. PROGRAM freezes candidate, bid type and public-only context. Some bids are cosmetic Hall feed records; approach/physical-bid can atomically move a candidate to the immediate USER/anchor vicinity, set status/map presentation and show a notification. A live presence may progress once after two anchor turns or three minutes, then exits within 12 minutes or on authority loss. No gift or relationship change is authorized.

Reachability: YES, but only through the successful direct-chat callback at index.html ~26146. Explicit public target text must match a narrow pattern. Ambient engagement needs three attention-eligible detail-chat replies in 12 minutes. Re-roll and item-use turns are excluded. Candidate and anchor must be physical same-Hall, not Away/Curator/Focus/Explore. Physical presence delivery additionally requires current foreground detail anchor, matching form, valid map entry/exit topology and no other active presence in that Hall. A scheduled 30-second reconciler runs on mount and visibility resume; this is retry/progression maintenance, not an autonomous producer.

Selection: public text freezes its named target; otherwise PROGRAM ranks candidates by USER affinity, public canonical relationship labels, personality trait keywords, 10-minute interaction recency and a stable hash. This is deterministic score ranking, not fair uniform pair sampling, and may favor familiar high-affinity residents repeatedly. Ambient Hall scene selection is separate and AI-owned.

Frequency: no intrinsic random roll. Without direct chat, 0 events per 100 Hall ticks. Even with direct chat, source-specific Hall/candidate cooldowns (ambient 45/90 minutes; explicit 5/15 minutes), pending/generating serialization, one active presence per Hall and foreground requirement reduce throughput. Durable operations are retained 48 hours/30 records. Entry records have type attention-bid with only candidate participantId and are display-only; the anchor exists in operation/presence metadata but is not a two-person authoritative Hall scene.

Relationship progression connection: NO. applyHallSceneRelationshipConsequences explicitly accepts only ambient and user-directed Hall scene records. Neither the attention-bid delivery nor presence progression calls it; prompts forbid claiming a relationship change. Later unrelated ambient scenes may include the same residents and then progress normally, but there is no direct consequence from Social Presence.

Regression assessment: current direct-chat enqueue and 30-second lifecycle are connected, and active-social/social-attention/homepage-ambient fixtures pass. No dead scheduler or refactor-induced disconnection was confirmed. The consequential disconnect is intentional by contract, but it explains why visible cat↔cat proximity yields no relationship gain. Do not convert every presence into a relationship delta; review a narrow PROGRAM-authored completed physical beat only if product wants this source to matter.
