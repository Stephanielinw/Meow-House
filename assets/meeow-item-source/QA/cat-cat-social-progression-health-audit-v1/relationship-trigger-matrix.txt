Positive social change is reachable through warmthDelta>0, trustDelta>0 or tensionDelta<0 when current tension>0. Negative social change is reachable through warmthDelta<0, trustDelta<0 or tensionDelta>0. There is no negative-only exclusion in validator or resolver. At bounds, requested change may resolve to zero. Frequency and valence bias are unknown because AI chooses optional scene and deltas, and no accepted-response sample/telemetry is present. The prompt deliberately says do not force conflict, affection or an update for routine presence; this may be appropriate but its quantitative effect is unmeasured.

Two residents in an accepted group scene automatically accumulate exposure, even if only one pair visibly exchanged. Three such scenes can increase familiarity for every pair. This should be reviewed for semantic fidelity before using exposure as a future meaningful-trend gate. The proposed gifting trend gate correctly excludes :program-familiarity events.
