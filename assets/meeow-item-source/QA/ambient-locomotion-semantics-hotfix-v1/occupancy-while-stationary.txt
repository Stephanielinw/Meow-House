Ambient Locomotion Semantics Hotfix V1 — occupancy while stationary

A supported stationary resident claims its current authored entry slot if that slot is free. The occupied map then prevents another resident from selecting that slot as a movement target. It has no target and contributes nothing to the reserved map.

If an entry slot is already occupied or reserved by another resident, the incoming resident remains represented as suspended with entry-slot-conflict; it does not steal occupancy. On a real posture change during movement, the target reservation is released before the current authoritative entry is reclaimed.

The focused fixture places a Sitting resident at slot a and a Standing resident at adjacent slot b. The Standing resident receives no-free-target, the Sitting resident keeps occupancy of a, a duplicate claimant stays in conflict, and reserved remains empty.
