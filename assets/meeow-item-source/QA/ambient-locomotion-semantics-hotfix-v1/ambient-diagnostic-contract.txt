Ambient Locomotion Semantics Hotfix V1 — diagnostic contract

Each visible Hall resident row reports spatialSupported, currentPose (visible presentation), authoritativePose (normalized structured Status or none), locomotionCapable, ambientState, suspensionReason, currentSlot, targetSlot, and moves. Foot and next-decision timing remain visible.

spatialSupported comes from the authored entry slot independent of posture. locomotionCapable requires a supported slot, CAT form, Hall availability, correct room, and authoritativePose === standing. A visually Standing legacy fallback with no structured posture therefore reports locomotionCapable false.

The one-shot prototype's eligibility rows are displayed only when that prototype flag is enabled. Ambient diagnostics do not label spatial support as movement eligibility.
