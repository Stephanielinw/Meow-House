Living Hall Spatial Foundation V1 — foot anchor

The moving resident has one transient foot coordinate. One positioned DOM wrapper carries its cat button/image, badge, and name; these children have local zero-position offsets, not separate world positions.
The existing Cat Renderer frame groundAnchor is translated by makeGroundAnchorPlacement(frame, 72) into the button's CSS anchor variables. The wrapper sits at the projected foot coordinate.
Travel preserves the currently authoritative Standing pose. V1 does not force Sitting after arrival: the current statusActivity/posture is owned by Status Sync, and a local pose override could contradict it. Sitting ground-anchor validation belongs in the later motion pass.
Collision tests use the foot only; image top-left, the 112x104 frame, and PNG alpha bounds are not collision inputs.
