Living Hall Spatial Foundation V1 — canonical coordinates

World authority is the authored room canvas: x and y in 1024x1024 map pixels. A resident's transient x/y denotes its foot point.
The module's projectFoot(room, foot, measuredContentBounds) returns CSS coordinates in the map stage's content box. measureStageContent reads clientWidth/clientHeight and the rendered stage rectangle; it excludes the stage border.
ResizeObserver and the window resize fallback update only measured bounds. Neither edits the foot point, resident save, logical mapPoint, or room metadata.
There is no inverse projection in V1. Viewport/global screen coordinates are not written as world state.
