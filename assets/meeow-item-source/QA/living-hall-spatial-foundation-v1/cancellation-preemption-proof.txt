Living Hall Spatial Foundation V1 — cancellation and preemption

The controller increments a generation token on cancel; delayed timer/frame callbacks check that token before publishing. Cancel also clears pending timer/frame handles.
The Hall integration cancels on room/Hall/tab/display-mode change, stage removal, resident unavailability or physical location change, document hiding, and Vue unmount. Once started, the prototype does not restart during the same page load.
Status preemption watches the selected resident's accepted lastStatusUpdateTime, not arbitrary rerenders or status text changes. Hall-scene preemption watches new records in accepted hallSceneRecords and requires the selected resident ID among participants in the prototype Hall. Other residents' scenes do not cancel it.
The app does not cancel, rewrite, or delay higher-authority events.
