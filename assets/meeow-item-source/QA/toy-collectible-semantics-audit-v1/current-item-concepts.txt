TOY / COLLECTIBLE SEMANTICS COVERAGE AUDIT V1 — CURRENT CONCEPTS
Design evidence only. No runtime data or semantic registry was changed.

METHOD / BOUNDARY
- Read production Shop, Mail, Away, Explore, Phone, item-visual registry, and the relevant fixtures. Retired third-party sprite metadata is excluded.
- Current repository seed data has four fixed toy products. User-authored products and AI-generated attachments/loot are open-ended; their examples below are evidence, not a claim about a private saved catalog.
- The fixed Explore location table currently has 47 locations and 101 comma-separated loot phrases. These are candidate prompts, not 101 simultaneously owned items. Explore/Mail accepted outputs freeze visualHint/visual per item. Runtime collectible items use type=collectible; this alone does not prove every object has the same behavior.
- visualHint is the existing appearance contract {object,material,form,context}; it is not Toy/Collectible gameplay semantics. "dynamic" below means no fixed hint/sprite for the phrase; the resolver may select one only after an actual item is generated.
- Registry-only baseitem archetypes are visual coverage, not proof that a named gameplay item exists.

AMBIGUOUS OR OUT OF SCOPE FOR AUTOMATIC TOY CLASSIFICATION
1. Generic "道具/产品/高奢物品/装备" Explore phrases do not identify an action or role. Do not assign play tags from the noun alone.
2. 猫薄荷 appears both as a deliberate Shop toy pouch and as raw herb/Explore loot. Packaging and category determine role; name/material alone do not.
3. 快船模型 may be a displayed souvenir or a playable toy. Current Explore guidance does not settle this.
4. 奢华零食, 神宴果实, 月桂花蜜 and other food-like Explore loot are not automatically Toy/Collectible semantic subjects, even if the current reward path records type=collectible.
5. Literal letters are documents/keepsakes, not toys and not automatically giftable. Mail attachment objects may be separately giftable when their canonical item type permits it.

AUTHORING / PHONE / OWNERSHIP EVIDENCE
- Shop authoring allows category=toy, type=consumable, name/description, price/effect, and frozen visual; current appraisal validates Food tags only. No Toy semantic registry or structured Toy properties exist.
- Phone Shop consumes the same Shop catalog. The legacy Phone delivery adapter is currently not exposed by Phone UI; its native source is explicitly distinct from Shop and does not establish a fixed new toy concept.
- Resident ownership fixture contains 绒球 toy and 小贝壳/纪念石 collectibles. These demonstrate transfer/identity, not any Toy semantic tags.
- Mail and Away attachments require name/description and closed visualHint. The fixture's generic 测试纪念品 with trinket/unknown/keepsake/travel is a placeholder, not a concrete interaction design.
