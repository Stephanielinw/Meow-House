Hall relationship feedback V1

Only an accepted shared Hall scene with an actually applied warmth, trust, or tension change receives one relationshipCue containing its canonical pair IDs. The Hall scene card shows a compact “关系发生了一点变化 · 查看” link. No raw numeric delta, popup, or interaction-wide toast is shown. Neutral and familiarity-only scenes receive no cue.
Clicking the cue opens the existing resident archive Relationship tab and its existing pair detail/history. The cue is normalized with Hall scene records so it survives ordinary save/reload. The summary row shows reverse-only direction explicitly as Target → Owner with the existing directional state label. Both directions remain separate; no synthetic affinity score is added.
