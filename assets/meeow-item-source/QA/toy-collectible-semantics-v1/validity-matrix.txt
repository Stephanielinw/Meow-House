CATEGORY-SPECIALIZED VALIDITY V1

semanticType toy (canonical category toy)
  role play: chase, bat, carry, sniff
  role comfort: carry, cuddle
  optional stimuli: rolling, swinging, fluttering, glowing, scented

semanticType collectible (canonical collectible/souvenir type or category)
  role keepsake: carry, observe
  role display: observe
  optional stimuli: glowing, scented
  rolling/swinging/fluttering are rejected as play-only stimuli.

Exactly one role and interaction; zero to two unique stimuli. Unknown, malformed, duplicate, category-conflicting, and cross-incompatible proposals are rejected in full. Food and literal letters cannot acquire this classification through validation. Missing or ambiguous semantics remain unclassified.
